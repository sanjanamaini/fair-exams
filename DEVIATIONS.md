# Deviations from the pre-registration

Every change to a registered analysis, and every analysis added after the pre-registration, is listed here with its date and reason. Registered analyses are still reported as registered; additions sit next to them, labelled as additions.

| Date | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-06 | **Addition A1: tail beyond the bulk.** For each centre, marks are converted to normal scores against the rest of its city; a normal location-scale fit to the centre's middle half (median and interquartile range) predicts its top-1% share; the observed count is tested against that prediction (randomised binomial z), calibrated with Efron's empirical null across centres, Benjamini-Hochberg at 0.05. | Designed **after** seeing that every registered flag is a shift of the whole distribution (lower quantiles shifted too), which is what sorting of a stronger cohort produces and what a boost to some candidates does not. A1 asks the fraud-specific question: more top scorers than the centre's own bulk predicts? Reported next to the registered flags, never instead of them. |
| 2026-10-06 | **Addition A2: where allotment is least random.** Per state, the intra-class correlation of marks among centres within cities, set against the share of the state's registrants who chose a regional-language paper (NTA's 2024 language table, spread over the states where each medium is offered). Spearman across the 12 states that offer a regional medium. | Designed **after** seeing that the ICC of marks (0.018) is far above that of top-1% status (0.0004), that centre deficits sit in coaching hubs, and that the Dantewada centre named a Hindi-medium school scores below its neighbour. The CBI record says the Godhra plotters had candidates choose Gujarati to land at their centre, and NTA prints a different booklet for each medium, so medium-based grouping was the leading explanation to test. |
| 2026-10-06 | **Addition A3: the comb.** The share of candidates attempting all 180 questions, estimated per 50-mark band (below 630) from the excess of candidates on multiples of 5 over the four neighbouring totals. | Seen in the national histogram while building Strand 3. Descriptive only; it shows how many candidates guess despite negative marking. Near the top, reachable totals are restricted by arithmetic, so those bands are excluded. |
| 2026-10-06 | **Addition A4: seed stability.** The screen is re-run with 50 seeds through the integrity monitor (same tests, deterministic parts computed once); every centre flagged under any seed is reported with the share of seeds that flag it. | Randomised p-values are exactly uniform under the null but make borderline flags depend on the draw. Building the monitor showed one borderline tail flag changing with the random stream. A reader needs to know which flags are solid. |
| 2026-10-06 | **Correction to the A2 reason above.** The CBI's stated reason for the Gujarati-medium choice at Godhra was to let local accomplices write in answers in Gujarati; candidates were also told to give local addresses. That the choice steered NTA's allotment towards the plotters' centre was an inference, not the CBI's statement. The report now says what the record says, and adds a fact from the data: Godhra had only two centres, both run by the same school group. | Found when checking sources before release. A2's analysis is unaffected; its motivation is corrected. |
| 2026-10-06 | Randomised z-scores now compute each tail directly. | Deep deficits rounded P(X > k) to 1 and produced infinite z-scores for the median statistic (S4). No registered flag changed. |
